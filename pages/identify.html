Tips for Accurate AI Diagnosis

Good Lighting

Ensure the plant is well-lit with natural indirect light. Avoid heavy shadows or washed-out flash.

Focus on Leaves & Symptoms

Get a sharp close-up of spots, discoloration, or wilted leaves, as well as the full plant outline.

Single Specimen

Frame one plant at a time in the center of the camera to give the AI the clearest view.
